I started doing game development just recently. And it had been a pleasant experience. I tried to use Raylib first, I used C++ with it and made a simple game called Riftfall. And later made a vanilla Javascript version of [Riftfall](https://riftfall.alexanderluo.com). That game was not good, I know.

And after some time, I tried to use Unreal, but it took at least 4 hours to compile, and never run. I had some hope in Godot, but I did not like the 'visual editor', so from that point onwards, I have been using ThreeJS for my game Nebulance.

## Inspiration

I got my inspiration from **The World**, **No Man's Sky**, **Minecraft** and **Myself**. 

I am sure that if you have been in the gaming community, you have heard about Minecraft. I used to play ***a ton of*** minecraft, many hours a day. 

No Man's Sky on the other hand is more niche, it is a Space RPG game, and Nebulance is also one.

Nebulance, originally started from the idea of a 'space simulation', it first had a generation system, that was seed-dependent, it meant the the same seed(e.g. 123) will always generate the same item.

## Gameplay & Features

Nebulance features a universe consisting of galaxies and stars. Space Stations are also there, for you to upgrade and sell loot.
